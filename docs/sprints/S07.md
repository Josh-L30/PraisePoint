# S07: Manual presenter and clean projector output

Status: Done, verified 2026-10-28. Base 690e4a8bba9ae69d4018a48724e61ea3032ce41f. Local frontend only; no new backend deployment or database migration.

Open a saved service, choose Review service slides → Present service, then Open projector. Move the new window to the projector; focus it and press F or use the browser fullscreen command. Output starts black until Send current slide. Current/next previews, paginated thumbnails, service-section jumps, previous/next, arrow/Space shortcuts and blackout/restore stay on the laptop. Controls stay available near the top of a short desktop viewport and while scrolling. Text inputs keep their editing keys; Space activates focused buttons without double advancement. Mobile directs volunteers to desktop presentation.

The operator builds one validated immutable service snapshot and verifies its files before enabling presentation. Cloud images are cached on the operator laptop through the existing authorized media service. Audience startup is a separate lazy entry without AuthProvider or a Supabase client: it reads only profile-bound local IndexedDB bytes matching the sent snapshot. The projector page contains the slide or black output, with no workspace shell, profile, buttons or notifications. Missing local files or font failure keep output black and report recovery to the operator.

BroadcastChannel uses an unpredictable session UUID on the same origin. Envelopes validate their version, session, audience nonce, profile, sequence, bounded transport and complete snapshot. Stale/duplicate/foreign messages are rejected. The first validated snapshot is retained by reference; changed service contents are rejected during that session. A new audience sends a join request but receives no slide until explicit Send current slide. Closing/reloading/reopening output resets that send requirement. Heartbeats detect a disconnected operator, and navigation/sign-out stops the session and releases its URLs. This is same-laptop communication, not remote or public sharing.

Background identity combines service-item section and media ID, preserving the actual video instance across lyric slides within that item, and replacing it intentionally at a section/media change. Backgrounds loop muted and continue behind blackout. Standalone video starts paused, has play/pause, volume, seek and restart commands, and produces audio only in the audience. Operator/thumbnail previews remain muted and paused. Blackout pauses and mutes a standalone clip; restore remains paused. Repeated restart commands retain distinct seek intent instead of accidentally resetting on volume changes. Rejected play/fullscreen promises report actionable recovery rather than claiming success. If browser audio is blocked, focus the projector window and press Space after requesting Play, or retry Play.
